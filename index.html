Research.

Unveiling Gender Bias in Social Media Comments: An NLP Analysis on Bangladeshi Influencers

An NLP-based research project that analyses gender bias, toxicity, abusive language and sentiment in YouTube comments aimed at Bangladeshi male and female influencers. It covers Bangla, English and Banglish (code-mixed) comments across five creator categories: Educational, Comedy, Food Vlogging, Lifestyle and Fitness.

I collected and preprocessed the comment data, annotated it and validated the ground truth, then applied machine learning and NLP techniques to classify sentiment and toxic comments. The study compares the nature and severity of negative comments directed at male and female influencers, including abusive, mocking, sarcastic and harassing content.
